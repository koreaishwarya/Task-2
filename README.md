# Task 2: Phishing Email Analysis

## Objective

Analyze a phishing email sample and identify indicators that suggest the email is malicious. This task helps develop the ability to recognize phishing attempts and understand common social engineering techniques used by attackers.

## Tools Used

* Email Sample (Text Format)
* Email Header Analyzer
* Web Browser
* Cybersecurity Research Resources

## Steps Performed

1. Obtained a sample phishing email for analysis.
2. Examined the sender's email address for signs of spoofing.
3. Analyzed email headers using an online header analysis tool.
4. Inspected embedded links and verified their actual destinations.
5. Checked for suspicious attachments.
6. Identified urgent or threatening language used to pressure the recipient.
7. Looked for mismatched URLs and deceptive domain names.
8. Reviewed the email for spelling and grammar mistakes.
9. Documented all phishing indicators discovered during the investigation.

## Phishing Indicators Identified

### 1. Suspicious Sender Address

* Sender domain appeared similar to a legitimate organization but contained subtle modifications.
* Possible email spoofing attempt detected.

### 2. Urgent Language

* The email created a sense of urgency by threatening account suspension or security issues.
* Designed to pressure the recipient into taking immediate action.

### 3. Suspicious Links

* Embedded hyperlinks pointed to domains different from the legitimate organization.
* Links could potentially lead to credential theft websites.

### 4. Generic Greeting

* Used generic greetings such as "Dear Customer" instead of the recipient's actual name.

### 5. Grammar and Spelling Errors

* Several grammatical mistakes and unusual wording were observed.

### 6. Header Anomalies

* Email authentication checks revealed inconsistencies.
* SPF, DKIM, or DMARC validation issues were identified.

### 7. Request for Sensitive Information

* The email attempted to obtain login credentials or personal information.

## Findings

The analyzed email exhibited multiple phishing characteristics, including sender spoofing, suspicious URLs, urgency tactics, and authentication failures. These indicators strongly suggest that the email was designed to deceive recipients and steal sensitive information.

## Conclusion

The email was determined to be a phishing attempt. Users should avoid clicking suspicious links, downloading unexpected attachments, or sharing sensitive information via email. Verifying sender authenticity and inspecting URLs carefully are essential steps in preventing phishing attacks.

## Learning Outcomes

* Understanding common phishing techniques.
* Identifying spoofed email addresses.
* Analyzing email headers for authenticity.
* Detecting malicious links and social engineering tactics.
* Improving cybersecurity awareness and threat detection skills.
